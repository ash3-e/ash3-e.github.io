About this background

Ten oscilloscope channels stacked vertically, all in P31 phosphor green, each one a real waveform you observed in lab.

Move your cursor anywhere — it acts as a measurement reticle, and rapid motion induces brief trigger jitter on every trace.

Channels

  • CH1 FG sine (Lab 3)
  • CH2 RC LP @ fc, −3 dB and π/4 lag (Lab 3)
  • CH3 Half-wave rectifier (Lab 5)
  • CH4 Bridge + smoothing cap, sawtooth ripple (Lab 5)
  • CH5 Diode clipper / Schmitt (Lab 5 / 9)
  • CH6 555 astable, asymmetric duty (Lab 9)
  • CH7 Op-amp integrator → triangle (Lab 8)
  • CH8 Relax. osc. VC, exp. charge/discharge (Lab 9)
  • CH9 Coax pulse + reflection echo (Lab 4)
  • CH10 Arduino PWM with drifting duty (Lab 11)

CRT effects in use

  • Phosphor persistence (low-α frame fade)
  • Three-pass beam: halo + mid + bright crisp center
  • Etched-glass graticule, 10×10 with 50-tick crosshair
  • Horizontal raster scanlines
  • Corner vignette darkening
  • Trigger marker (▶ T on CH6)
  • Crosshair reticle on cursor
  • Status line: 1 ms/div · 200 mV/div
