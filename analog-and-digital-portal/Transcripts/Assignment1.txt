Key equations

$$P=\frac{\Delta U}{\Delta t}=\frac{\Delta qV}{\Delta t}=IV=I^{2}R$$

$$I=\sqrt{P/R}$$

$$V=IR$$

$$R_{TOT}=R_{1}+R_{2}=1500\ \Omega$$

$$V=\sqrt{\frac{0.25\,\text{W}}{1000\,\Omega}}\cdot 1500\ \Omega\approx 23.7\ \text{V}$$

$$V_{th}=V\left(\frac{R_{2}}{R_{1}+R_{2}}\right)=10\cdot\frac{5}{8}=6.25\ \text{V}$$

$$R_{\|eq}=\frac{R_{1}R_{2}}{R_{1}+R_{2}}=\frac{3\cdot 5}{3+5}=1.875\ \Omega$$

$$R_{th}=R_{\|eq}+2\ \Omega=3.875\ \Omega$$

$$V_{out}=\frac{R_{L}}{R_{th}+R_{L}}V_{th}=\frac{10}{13.875}\cdot 6.25\approx 4.50\ \text{V}$$

$$I_{V}=\frac{V-V_{A}}{R_{3}}=\frac{V-V_{R_{2}}}{R_{3}}$$

$$IR_{2}R_{3}+VR_{2}=V_{A}(R_{2}+R_{3})$$

$$V_{R_{2}}=\frac{R_{2}(IR_{3}+V)}{R_{2}+R_{3}}$$

$$I_{V}=\frac{VR_{2}+VR_{3}-R_{2}R_{3}I-R_{2}V}{R_{3}(R_{2}+R_{3})}=\frac{V-IR_{2}}{R_{2}+R_{3}}$$

$$R_{in}^{(no\ load)}=26+35=61\ \Omega$$

$$R_{eq\|}=\frac{35\cdot(26+50)}{35+26+50}\approx 24\ \Omega$$

$$R_{in}^{(load)}=26+24=50\ \Omega$$

$$I_{tot}=\frac{V}{R_{tot}}=\frac{2}{100}=0.02\ \text{A}$$

$$V_{in}=I_{tot}\cdot R_{in}=0.02\cdot 50=1\ \text{V}$$

$$V_{L}=V_{in}\cdot\frac{R_{\|}}{R_{S}+R_{\|}}=1\cdot\frac{24}{50}=0.48\ \text{V}$$

$$P_{L}=\frac{V_{L}^{2}}{R_{L}}=\frac{0.48^{2}}{50}\approx 4.6\ \text{mW}$$

$$P_{L,\text{direct}}=\frac{V_{L,d}^{2}}{R_{L}}=\frac{1^{2}}{50}=20\ \text{mW},\qquad \text{attenuation}=\frac{P_{L}}{P_{L,\text{direct}}}\approx 0.23$$
