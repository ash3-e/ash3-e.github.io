Key equations

$$\text{Diode condition: }I>0\Leftrightarrow V>V_{D}\approx 0.6\ \text{V (Si)}$$

$$I(V)=I_{sat}(e^{eV/k_{B}T}-1),\quad I=0\Leftrightarrow V<V_{D}$$

$$V_{out,\text{bridge}}(t)=|V_{in}(t)|-2V_{D}\quad\text{(full-wave rectifier output)}$$

$$\text{Half-wave w/ filter: }V_{out}=(V_{in}-0.6)\,e^{-t/RC}\approx V_{max}\!\left(1-\frac{t}{RC}\right)$$

$$\text{Discharge time: for }R\gg \frac{2\pi}{\omega},\ e^{-t/RC}\approx 1-\frac{t}{RC}$$

$$\Delta V=\text{ripple}=\text{peak-to-peak}$$

$$\Delta V=V_{max}\!\left(\frac{\Delta t}{RC}\right)=V_{max}\!\left(\frac{2\pi}{\omega RC}\right)=\frac{I_{max}}{\omega C}\!\cdot 2\pi=\frac{I_{max}}{fC}\quad(f=\text{frequency})$$

$$\text{BJT (npn): }I_{B}+I_{C}=I_{E},\quad I_{C}=\alpha I_{E},\quad \alpha\approx 1$$

$$I_{B}=(1-\alpha)I_{E},\qquad \beta=\frac{\alpha}{1-\alpha}\gg 1$$

$$\beta\approx 50\text{–}200\ \text{for typical transistors in our lab}$$

$$I_{C}=I_{E}=\beta I_{B}\quad\text{(active region)}$$

$$\text{Active condition: }V_{BE}>0.6\ \text{V}\ \text{and}\ V_{CE}>V_{BE}$$

$$\text{Some }e^{-}\text{ recombine with holes in base creating }I_{B}$$

$$\text{Some }e^{-}\text{ are pushed from B to C by applied }V_{CE}\text{ and create }I_{C}$$

$$\text{Goal: }I_{C}\gg I_{B},\ I_{E}=I_{B}+I_{C}\approx I_{C}\quad\text{(to make a good amp)}$$

$$\text{Switch example: }V_{S}=10\ \text{V, light bulb }R_{L}=500\ \Omega,\ V_{in}=0\ \text{V or }5\ \text{V}$$

$$V_{in}=0\Rightarrow I_{B}=0\Rightarrow I_{C}=I_{L}=0\ \text{(transistor off)}$$

$$\text{For }V_{in}=5\ \text{V: assume }V_{BE}=0.6\ \text{V, }I_{B}=\frac{V_{in}-V_{BE}}{4.4\ \text{k}\Omega}=\frac{4.4\ \text{V}}{4.4\ \text{k}\Omega}=1\ \text{mA}$$

$$I_{C}=\beta I_{B}=100\ \text{mA}\Rightarrow\text{max voltage across }R_{L}\approx 10\ \text{V}$$

$$I_{\max}=\frac{V_{S}}{R_{L}}=\frac{10\ \text{V}}{500\ \Omega}=20\ \text{mA},\qquad I_{B}=20\ \mu\text{A}$$

$$\text{Emitter Follower: assume }V_{in}>0.6\ \text{V and }\frac{V_{E}}{R}\gg \beta I_{B}\Rightarrow \text{active region}$$

How to use this page
Read each displayed relation as solving one of four jobs: replacing each diode in a bridge or half-wave rectifier by an "on" 0.6 V drop or an open circuit depending on V > V_D, computing the smoothing-cap ripple ΔV from the discharge slope between successive peaks, applying the BJT current relations (β = I_C/I_B, I_E ≈ I_C in active mode), or sliding the transistor between off / active / saturated by checking whether V_BE clears 0.6 V and whether V_CE clears V_BE.
