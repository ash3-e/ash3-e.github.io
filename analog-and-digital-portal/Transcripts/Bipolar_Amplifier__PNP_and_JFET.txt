Bipolar Amplifier, PNP, and JFET
Overview
Single-page lecture notes that bring together the bipolar transistor amplifier (NPN active region, gain G = −R₁/R₂), input and output impedances of the emitter follower, the PNP variant, the resistor-divider bias circuit needed to put a single-supply amplifier into its active region, and the field-effect (JFET) transistor with its gate-controlled drain-source channel and its constant-current-source application. The page emphasises the contrast between BJTs (current-driven, β = I_C/I_B ≈ 200) and JFETs (voltage-driven, gate draws no DC current). Key equations

$$I_{B}+I_{C}=I_{E},\qquad I_{B}\ll I_{C}\approx I_{E}$$

$$I_{C}=\beta I_{B},\qquad \beta\gg 1\ \text{and independent of }V_{CE}$$

$$\beta=\frac{I_{C}}{I_{B}}=\frac{2\,\text{mA}}{10\,\mu\text{A}}=\frac{2\!\cdot\!10^{-3}}{10\!\cdot\!10^{-6}}=200$$

$$\text{Emitter follower: }V_{BE}=0.6\ \text{V},\ V_{out}=V_{in}-0.6\ \text{V}$$

$$Z_{in}=\frac{V_{B}}{I_{B}}=\frac{V_{in}}{I_{in}}=(\beta+1)R_{L}\approx \beta R_{L}\quad\text{(input impedance)}$$

$$\frac{\Delta V_{B}}{\Delta I_{B}}=\frac{dV_{B}}{dI_{B}}=Z_{in},\qquad V_{B}=Z_{in}\,I_{B}$$

$$Z_{out}=-\frac{dV_{E}}{dI_{E}}\approx\frac{R_{0}}{1+\beta}\quad\text{(transistor lowers Z_out by }\beta\text{)}$$

$$V_{S}-V_{out}=(I_{C}+I_{L})R_{1},\quad V_{out}=V_{S}-I_{C}R_{1}$$

$$V_{E}=V_{in}-0.6\ \text{V},\qquad I_{E}=\frac{V_{E}}{R_{2}}=\frac{V_{in}-0.6}{R_{2}}$$

$$I_{C}\approx I_{E}=\frac{V_{in}-0.6\ \text{V}+V_{S}}{R_{2}}\quad\text{(when }R_{2}\text{ is the emitter resistor)}$$

$$V_{out}=\left(-\frac{R_{1}}{R_{2}}\right)V_{in}+0.6V\!\left(\frac{R_{1}}{R_{2}}\right)+V_{S}\!\left(-\frac{R_{1}}{R_{2}}\right)$$

$$V_{out}=G\,V_{in}+\text{const},\qquad G=-\frac{R_{1}}{R_{2}}\quad\text{(amplifier gain)}$$

$$\text{Clipping: }|V_{out}|\to V_{S}\Rightarrow V_{out}\ \text{flat-tops at }\pm V_{S}$$

$$V_{B}=\frac{R_{4}}{R_{3}+R_{4}}V_{S}\quad\text{(bias divider)}$$

$$V_{out,\text{rest}}=\frac{V_{S}}{2}\quad\text{(by choosing ratio }R_{3}/R_{4}\text{)}$$

$$\text{Assume }V_{in}\text{ is AC and }\omega\gg \frac{1}{R_{3}\!\|\!R_{4}\,C_{in}}\ \text{(coupling capacitor)}$$

$$\text{JFET symbol convention: arrow on gate; n-channel vs p-channel}$$

$$\Delta V=|V_{p}|+V_{BS}\quad\text{(channel voltage at junction)}$$

$$\frac{dV}{dI}=R\quad\text{(ohmic region)},\qquad V=IR$$

$$V_{BS}\!\uparrow\Rightarrow R_{BS}\!\uparrow\Rightarrow I_{d}\approx\text{const as }V_{BS}\text{ changes (saturation)}$$

$$\text{JFET current source: }I_{D}=I_{d,\text{sat}}\ \text{set by }V_{GS},\ \text{gate draws no current }(I_{G}=0)$$
