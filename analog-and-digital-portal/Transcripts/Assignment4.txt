Assignment 4
Overview
Homework on transmission lines and reflections at a load: matching a 50 Ω cable into a 75 Ω instrument by adding a parallel matching element, and analyzing a 1 MHz, 1 V wave on a 50 Ω cable terminated by a series RC of R = 25 Ω and C = 10 µF. The second problem walks through the reflection coefficient, the reflected voltage and current amplitudes and phases, the voltage across the resistor, and an energy audit between incident, reflected, and dissipated average power. This transcript is a page-specific reconstruction for preview and search. It is meant to read like a cleaned study guide, not like raw OCR.

Problem coverage
The set is centered on impedance matching and reflection bookkeeping. The pages clearly include the parallel impedance condition Z₀ = Z_M ∥ Z_L solved for Z_M = 125 Ω so the line sees 50 Ω at the junction, the recognition that at 1 MHz a 10 µF capacitor has Z_C ≈ 0.016j Ω which is negligible compared to 25 Ω so Z_L ≈ R, the reflection coefficient Γ = (Z_L − Z₀)/(Z_L + Z₀) = −1/3 with amplitude 1/3 and phase 180°, the corresponding reflected current with phase 0°, the load voltage V_L = V₀(1+Γ) leading to V_R ≈ 0.6667 V, and the three-way power split with P_in = V₀²/(2Z₀) = 10 mW, P_ref = Γ²P_in = 10/9 mW, and P_R = (1/2)I_L²R ≈ 8.89 mW. The assignment is using these to lock in the same conservation idea: P_in = P_ref + P_dissipated.

Key equations

$$\frac{1}{Z_{0}}=\frac{1}{Z_{M}}+\frac{1}{Z_{L}}\ \Rightarrow\ Z_{M}=\frac{Z_{0}Z_{L}}{Z_{L}-Z_{0}}$$

$$50=\frac{75\,Z_{M}}{75+Z_{M}}\ \Rightarrow\ 25Z_{M}=3750\ \Rightarrow\ Z_{M}=125\ \Omega$$

$$Z_{C}=\frac{1}{j\omega C}$$

$$Z_{C}\big|_{\omega=2\pi\cdot 10^{6},\,C=10\,\mu\text{F}}=\frac{1}{j\cdot 2\pi\cdot 10^{6}\cdot 10^{-5}}\approx 0.0159\,j\ \Omega\ \text{(negligible)}$$

$$Z_{L}=R+Z_{C}\approx R=25\ \Omega$$

$$\Gamma=\frac{Z_{L}-Z_{0}}{Z_{L}+Z_{0}}=\frac{25-50}{25+50}=-\frac{1}{3}$$

$$|V_{R,\text{refl}}|=|\Gamma|V_{0}=\tfrac{1}{3}\ \text{V},\qquad \text{phase }=180^{\circ}$$

$$I_{0}=\frac{V_{0}}{Z_{0}}=\frac{1}{50}\ \text{A}$$

$$|I_{R,\text{refl}}|=|\Gamma|I_{0}=\tfrac{1}{50}\cdot\tfrac{1}{3}=\frac{1}{150}\approx 0.00667\ \text{A},\qquad \text{phase }=0^{\circ}$$

$$V_{L}=V_{0}(1+\Gamma)=1\!\left(1-\tfrac{1}{3}\right)=\tfrac{2}{3}\ \text{V}$$

$$I_{L}=\frac{V_{L}}{Z_{L}}=\frac{2/3}{25}\approx 0.0266\ \text{A}$$

$$V_{R}=I_{L}R=\frac{V_{L}}{Z_{L}}\cdot R\approx \frac{2/3}{25}\cdot 25=\tfrac{2}{3}\approx 0.667\ \text{V}$$

$$P_{in}=\frac{V_{0}^{2}}{2Z_{0}}=\frac{1}{100}\ \text{W}=10\ \text{mW}$$

$$P_{ref}=\Gamma^{2}P_{in}=\tfrac{1}{9}\cdot 10\ \text{mW}=\tfrac{10}{9}\ \text{mW}\approx 1.11\ \text{mW}$$

$$P_{R}=\tfrac{1}{2}I_{L}^{2}R=\tfrac{1}{2}(0.0266)^{2}(25)\approx 8.89\ \text{mW}$$

$$P_{in}=P_{ref}+P_{R}\ \text{(energy conservation check)}$$

$$\text{Match condition: }Z_{M}\,\|\,Z_{L}=Z_{0}\ \Rightarrow\ \Gamma=0$$

$$\text{General load: }Z_{L}=Z_{R}+Z_{C}$$

$$\omega=2\pi f=2\pi\!\times\!10^{6}\ \text{rad/s}$$

$$|Z_{L}|=\sqrt{Z_{R}^{2}+Z_{C}^{2}}\approx\sqrt{25^{2}+0.016^{2}}\approx 25\ \Omega$$

$$V_{L}=V_{0}(1+\Gamma),\qquad I_{L}=V_{0}(1-\Gamma)/Z_{0}\ \text{(traveling wave on terminated line)}$$
