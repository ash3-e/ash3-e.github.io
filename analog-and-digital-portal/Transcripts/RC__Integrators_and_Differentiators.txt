RC Integrators and Differentiators
Overview
Single-page lecture notes that build first-order RC and RL transients (charging and discharging a capacitor through a resistor, discharging an inductor), then move on to passive integrator and differentiator circuits, and close with AC steady-state analysis using complex impedance Z_C = 1/(iωC), Z_L = iωL with the same series/parallel rules used for resistors. The page emphasises the time constant τ = RC (or L/R), the universal exponential form V(t) = V(∞) + (V(0) − V(∞))e^(−t/τ), and the slow-input vs fast-input limits that pick which circuit acts as integrator and which acts as differentiator. Key equations

$$\text{Charging loop: }V_{0}-I(t)R-V_{C}(t)=0,\quad I(t)=C\frac{dV_{C}}{dt}$$

$$V_{0}-RC\frac{dV_{C}}{dt}-V_{C}(t)=0,\qquad V_{C}(0)=Q(0)/C=0$$

$$\text{Guess: }V_{C}(t)=A+B\,e^{-t/\tau}$$

$$V_{C}(0)=0\Rightarrow A=-B,\qquad t\to\infty:e^{-t/\tau}\to 0\Rightarrow A=V_{0}$$

$$V_{C}(t)=V_{0}\!\left(1-e^{-t/RC}\right),\qquad V_{C}(RC)=0.63\,V_{0}$$

$$\frac{dV_{C}}{dt}=-A\!\left(-\tfrac{1}{\tau}\right)e^{-t/\tau}=\frac{A}{\tau}e^{-t/\tau}=\frac{V_{0}}{RC}e^{-t/RC}$$

$$V_{R}(t)=I(t)R=V_{0}\,e^{-t/RC},\qquad RC=\text{time constant}$$

$$\text{Discharging inductor (switch open): }-L\frac{dI}{dt}-IR=0\Rightarrow \frac{dI}{dt}=-\frac{R}{L}I$$

$$\int_{I_{0}}^{I(t)}\frac{dI'}{I'}=\int_{0}^{t}-\frac{R}{L}\,dt'\Rightarrow\ln\frac{I(t)}{I_{0}}=-\frac{R}{L}t$$

$$I(t)=I_{0}\,e^{-Rt/L},\qquad I(0)=I_{0}$$

$$\text{Switched RC example: at }t=0\ \text{closed},\ V_{0}\to V_{C}=V_{0};\ I_{1}=V_{0}/2R,\ I_{2}=V_{0}/R$$

$$\text{At }t=3RC-\Delta t:V_{0}\approx V_{A};\ \text{at }t=3RC+\Delta t:\text{after flip}$$

$$\text{Integrator (passive): }V_{in}-V_{C}-V_{out}=0,\ \frac{dV_{in}}{dt}=\frac{V_{out}}{RC}+\frac{dV_{out}}{dt}$$

$$\text{If }V_{in}\text{ varies fast }(T\ll RC):\ V_{out}\propto\int V_{in}\,dt$$

$$RC\frac{dV_{out}}{dt}\propto \omega RC\sin(\omega t),\qquad \omega RC\gg 1$$

$$\text{Differentiator: }V_{in}-V_{C}-V_{out}=0;\ \text{if }V_{in}(t)\text{ varies slowly on timescale }RC$$

$$V_{out}=\cos(\omega t)\Rightarrow \frac{dV_{out}}{dt}=\omega\sin(\omega t)\quad\text{(when }V_{in}\propto V_{out})$$

$$I(t)=C\frac{dV}{dt}=-CV_{0}\omega\sin(\omega t)=CV_{0}\omega\cos(\omega t+\pi/2)$$

$$\text{Complex form: }\hat v(t)=\hat I(t)\,Z_{C},\quad Z_{C}=\frac{1}{i\omega C}$$

$$\hat v(t)=V_{0}e^{i\omega t},\qquad \hat I(t)=C\frac{d\hat v}{dt}=i\omega C\,\hat v(t)$$

$$\text{Inductor: }\hat v(t)=L\frac{d\hat I}{dt},\quad \hat I(t)=\frac{V_{0}}{i\omega L}e^{i\omega t},\quad Z_{L}=i\omega L$$

$$\text{Series: }Z_{eq}=Z_{1}+Z_{2}\qquad \text{Parallel: }\frac{1}{Z_{eq}}=\frac{1}{Z_{1}}+\frac{1}{Z_{2}}$$
