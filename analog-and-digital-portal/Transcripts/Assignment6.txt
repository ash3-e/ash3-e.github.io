Assignment 6
Overview

Key equations

$$V_{E}=V_{B}-V_{BE}=V_{B}-0.6\ \text{V}$$

$$V_{E}=1.5-0.6=0.9\ \text{V}$$

$$I_{E}=\frac{V_{E}}{R_{E}}=\frac{0.9}{100}=8\ \text{mA}$$

$$I_{E}=I_{C}+I_{B},\qquad I_{C}=\beta I_{B}$$

$$I_{E}=(\beta+1)I_{B}\ \Rightarrow\ I_{B}=\frac{I_{E}}{\beta+1}=\frac{8\,\text{mA}}{201}\approx 39.8\ \mu\text{A}$$

$$I_{C}=\beta I_{B}=200(39.8\,\mu\text{A})\approx 7.96\ \text{mA}$$

$$V_{CE}=V_{C}-V_{E}=10-0.9=9.1\ \text{V}\ \Rightarrow\ V_{CE}>V_{BE}\ \text{(active region)}$$

$$V_{B}=\frac{R_{4}}{R_{3}+R_{4}}V_{S}=\frac{1\,\text{k}\Omega}{3\,\text{k}\Omega+1\,\text{k}\Omega}\cdot 10\ \text{V}=2.5\ \text{V}$$

$$V_{E}=V_{B}-0.6=1.9\ \text{V},\qquad I_{E}=\frac{V_{E}}{R_{2}}=\frac{1.9}{100}=19\ \text{mA}$$

$$I_{C}\approx I_{E}=19\ \text{mA}\quad\text{(large }\beta\text{)}$$

$$V_{C}=V_{S}-I_{C}R_{1}=10-(19\,\text{mA})(300\,\Omega)=4.3\ \text{V}$$

$$\text{Single follower: }V_{out}=V_{in}-0.6\ \text{V},\qquad \text{DC offset}=0.6\ \text{V}$$

$$\text{Darlington: }V_{B_{2}}=V_{E_{1}}=V_{in}-0.6\ \text{V}$$

$$V_{out}=V_{E_{2}}=V_{B_{2}}-0.6\ \text{V}=V_{in}-1.2\ \text{V},\qquad \text{DC offset}=1.2\ \text{V}$$

$$Z_{in}=\frac{V_{in}}{I_{in}}=\frac{dV_{in}}{dI_{in}}$$

$$I_{B}=\frac{I_{E}}{\beta+1}\ \Rightarrow\ Z_{in}^{(\text{single})}=\frac{V_{in}}{I_{B}}\approx\frac{V_{in}(\beta+1)R_{L}}{V_{in}-0.6}\approx (\beta+1)R_{L}$$

$$\text{Darlington: }I_{E_{1}}=I_{R}+I_{B_{2}}\ \text{with }I_{R}=\frac{V_{E_{1}}}{R}\approx\frac{V_{in}-0.6}{R}$$

$$\text{If }R\gg \beta R_{L}:\ I_{E_{1}}\approx I_{B_{2}},\qquad I_{B_{1}}=\frac{I_{E_{1}}}{\beta+1}\approx\frac{I_{B_{2}}}{\beta}$$

$$Z_{in}^{(\text{Darlington})}=\frac{dV_{B}}{dI_{B}}=\beta\!\cdot\!\beta R_{L}=\beta^{2}R_{L}$$

$$\text{Active region condition: }V_{CE}>V_{CE,\text{sat}}\ \text{(roughly }>V_{BE}\text{)}$$

How to use this page
Read each displayed relation as solving one of four jobs: pinning the base voltage from a divider, dropping 0.6 V to the emitter, converting the emitter voltage into a current through R_E, or chaining (β+1) and β to convert between base, collector, and emitter currents. That is the logic tying the handwritten algebra together.
