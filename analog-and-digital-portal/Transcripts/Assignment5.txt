Assignment 5
Overview
Homework on diode circuits and rudimentary diode logic: finding the maximum input voltage for which a diode in a three-resistor network draws no current (assuming an ideal 0.6 V drop), sketching the output of an AC-driven clipper for two different input amplitudes (4 V and 0.5 V), and identifying which of two two-input diode circuits behaves as a logical AND and which behaves as a logical OR by enumerating outputs over all 0 V / 5 V input combinations. This transcript is a page-specific reconstruction for preview and search. It is meant to read like a cleaned study guide, not like raw OCR.

Problem coverage
The set is centered on switching behavior of diodes. The pages clearly include the diode-off threshold computed by replacing the diode with the open-circuit / 0.6 V clamp model and applying a simple voltage divider through R₂₃ = R₂ + R₃, two waveform sketches where a 4 V cosine is clipped between roughly +1.1 V and −1.4 V by three diodes one way and two diodes the other while a 0.5 V cosine sits unclipped because the threshold sums of 1.8 V (3D) and 1.2 V (2D) are never reached, and the AND/OR identification where the resistor-pulled-up circuit reads HIGH only when both inputs are HIGH and the resistor-pulled-down circuit reads HIGH whenever any input is HIGH. The assignment is using these to set up the same diode mental model: a diode is a one-way switch with a fixed forward drop.

Key equations

$$\text{Ideal diode model: }I_{D}>0\Leftrightarrow V_{D}=V_{\text{drop}}=0.6\ \text{V},\qquad I_{D}=0\Leftrightarrow V_{D}<0.6\ \text{V}$$

$$\text{When clamped: }V_{D}=0.6\ \text{V (node A held by diode)}$$

$$V_{A}=V_{0}\!\left(\frac{R_{23}}{R_{1}+R_{2}+R_{3}}\right)\quad\text{(divider with diode off)}$$

$$V_{0,\max}=V_{A}\!\left(\frac{R_{1}+R_{2}+R_{3}}{R_{23}}\right)\quad\text{evaluated at }V_{A}=0.6\ \text{V}$$

$$R_{23}=R_{2}+R_{3}$$

$$\text{Series 3 diodes drop: }3V_{D}=1.8\ \text{V}$$

$$\text{Series 2 diodes drop: }2V_{D}=1.2\ \text{V}$$

$$\text{Clip range (4 V cosine): }+1.1\ \text{V to }-1.4\ \text{V}$$

$$V_{in}(t)=4\cos(\omega t)\ \text{Volts}\Rightarrow V_{out}\ \text{clipped when }|V_{in}|>\ \text{diode-stack drop}$$

$$V_{in}(t)=0.5\cos(\omega t)\ \text{Volts}\Rightarrow V_{out}=V_{in}\ \text{(amplitude below 1.2 V/1.8 V threshold)}$$

$$\text{Pull-up (AND) circuit: }V_{out}=5\ \text{V iff both }V_{in,1},V_{in,2}=5\ \text{V}$$

$$\text{Pull-down (OR) circuit: }V_{out}=5\ \text{V iff either }V_{in,1}\ \text{or }V_{in,2}=5\ \text{V}$$

$$\text{AND truth: }(0,0)\!\to\!0,\ (5,0)\!\to\!0,\ (0,5)\!\to\!0,\ (5,5)\!\to\!5$$

$$\text{OR truth: }(0,0)\!\to\!0,\ (5,0)\!\to\!5,\ (0,5)\!\to\!5,\ (5,5)\!\to\!5$$

$$\text{Through-resistor when diode is off: }I_{R}=0\Rightarrow V_{R}=0\Rightarrow V_{out}=V_{in}$$

$$V_{out}=V_{in}-V_{D,\text{stack}}\quad\text{when diode stack conducts}$$

$$V_{D,\text{forward}}\approx 0.6\ \text{V (Si)},\qquad V_{D,\text{reverse}}\approx 0$$

$$V_{out,\max}^{(3D)}=+1.8\ \text{V},\qquad V_{out,\min}^{(3D)}=-1.2\ \text{V}\ \text{(asymmetric stack)}$$

$$\text{KVL clamp: }V_{in}=V_{out}+nV_{D}\ \text{when stack of }n\ \text{diodes conducts}$$

$$P_{D}=V_{D}I_{D}\quad\text{(power dissipated in conducting diode)}$$

$$\text{Logic levels: LOW }=0\ \text{V},\quad \text{HIGH }=5\ \text{V}$$

How to use this page
Read each displayed relation as solving one of four jobs: replacing the diode with the right model (open circuit / 0.6 V clamp / forward conducting), applying a voltage divider once that replacement is in, summing diode drops along a clipping path to find the output ceiling and floor, or tabulating logic outputs by walking through every input combination. That is the logic tying the handwritten algebra together.
