Assignment 3
Overview
Homework on resonant series RLC behavior and on cascaded passive RC filters: locating the resonance frequency and quality factor of a series 2 µF / 3 Ω / 2 mH branch, evaluating the impedance at resonance, designing a single-stage RC low-pass filter that attenuates a 1 kHz signal by a factor of ten, and analyzing what happens when the output of that low-pass is fed into a second identical stage. This transcript is a page-specific reconstruction for preview and search. It is meant to read like a cleaned study guide, not like raw OCR.

Problem coverage
The set is centered on resonance and frequency response. The pages clearly include the resonance condition Im[Z_eq] = 0 for a series RLC giving ω₀ = 1/√(LC), the quality factor Q = ω₀/Δω with Δω = R/L for the series circuit, the impedance reduction at resonance to Z = R, an attenuation-by-ten requirement for a 1 kHz low-pass filter that fixes the product RC, and a two-stage cascaded RC low-pass where the equivalent input impedance, the squared transfer function, and the log-log shape against a single-stage filter must all be drawn. The assignment is using these to build the same intuition twice: the pole structure of |H(ω)|² controls both the cutoff and the slope.

Key equations

$$Z_{eq}=R+\frac{1}{i\omega C}+i\omega L=R+i\!\left(\omega L-\frac{1}{\omega C}\right)$$

$$\text{Im}[Z_{eq}]=\omega L-\frac{1}{\omega C}=0\ \Rightarrow\ \omega_{0}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{LC}}$$

$$\omega_{0}=\big[(2\!\times\!10^{-3})(2\!\times\!10^{-6})\big]^{-1/2}=5000\sqrt{10}\approx 1.58\!\times\!10^{4}\ \text{rad/s}$$

$$f_{0}=\frac{\omega_{0}}{2\pi}=\frac{5000\sqrt{10}}{2\pi}\approx 2.52\!\times\!10^{3}\ \text{Hz}$$

$$Q=\frac{\omega_{0}}{\Delta\omega},\qquad \Delta\omega=\frac{R}{L}$$

$$Q=\frac{\omega_{0}L}{R}=\frac{(5000\sqrt{10})(2\!\times\!10^{-3})}{3}\approx 10.54$$

$$|Z|=\sqrt{R^{2}+(X_{L}-X_{C})^{2}}$$

$$\text{At resonance: }X_{L}=X_{C}\ \Rightarrow\ Z=R=3\ \Omega$$

$$g=\frac{|V_{out}|}{|V_{in}|}=\frac{V_{C}}{V_{series}}=\frac{IX_{C}}{IZ_{series}}=\frac{1/\omega C}{\sqrt{R^{2}+(1/\omega C)^{2}}}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{1+\omega^{2}R^{2}C^{2}}}$$

$$\frac{1}{10}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{1+\omega^{2}R^{2}C^{2}}}\ \Rightarrow\ 99=\omega^{2}R^{2}C^{2}\ \Rightarrow\ \sqrt{99}=\omega RC$$

$$RC=\frac{\sqrt{99}}{2\pi(1000)}\approx 1.58\ \text{ms}$$

$$\text{Choose }R=158\ \text{k}\Omega,\ C=1.0\,\mu\text{F}\ \Rightarrow\ f_{c}=\frac{1}{2\pi RC}=\frac{1}{2\pi(1.58\!\times\!10^{-3})}\approx 100.73\ \text{Hz}$$

$$Z_{c}=\frac{1}{i\omega C}$$

$$Z_{node\,1}=Z_{C}\,\|\,(R+Z_{C})$$

$$Z_{in}=R+\big[Z_{C}\,\|\,(R+Z_{C})\big]$$

$$H(\omega)=\frac{V_{out}}{V_{in}}=\frac{1}{1+3(i\omega RC)+(i\omega RC)^{2}}$$

$$\left|\frac{V_{out}}{V_{in}}\right|^{2}=\frac{1}{(1-x^{2})^{2}+(3x)^{2}}=\frac{1}{1+7x^{2}+x^{4}},\qquad x=\omega RC$$

$$\text{Single RC: }|H|^{2}=\frac{1}{1+x^{2}},\qquad \text{Double RC: }|H|^{2}=\frac{1}{1+7x^{2}+x^{4}}$$

$$\text{Low }\omega:\ |H|^{2}\to 1,\qquad \text{High }\omega:\ |H|^{2}\to 0\ \text{(40 dB/decade for double)}$$

$$\omega\ll 1/RC\Rightarrow G\to 1\ \text{(low pass)},\qquad \omega\gg 1/RC\Rightarrow G\to 0$$

How to use this page
Read each displayed relation as solving one of four jobs: writing the series impedance, locating the frequency where its imaginary part vanishes, building the magnitude of a transfer function from the same reactance pieces, or chaining two filters by combining impedances first and then squaring the result. That is the logic tying the handwritten algebra together.
