Lab 5: Diode Circuits and Rectifiers
Overview
Lab on the practical use of silicon diodes (1N914) for rectification and waveform shaping. The student first identifies the cathode and anode with the DMM diode-test (forward V_D ≈ 0.6 V, reverse essentially open), builds a half-wave rectifier from a 6.3 V transformer with optional 10 µF / 100 µF electrolytic smoothing capacitor, measures the ripple and confirms the ripple-formula scaling ΔV ≈ I_load/(fC), then builds a full-wave bridge rectifier and shows that the ripple frequency doubles to 120 Hz. The lab ends on diode logic gates (an AND and an OR built from two 1N914s and a pull-up/pull-down resistor) and a Zener-style voltage clipper that flat-tops a 4 V_pp sine. This transcript is a page-specific reconstruction for preview and search.

Problem coverage
The set is centered on rectification and the I-V approximation of a Si diode. The pages clearly include the diode model "ON" when V_anode − V_cathode > V_D ≈ 0.6 V (resistance ≈ 0, current limited by series components) and "OFF" otherwise (open circuit, I ≈ 0), the half-wave rectifier where V_out(t) = max(V_in(t) − V_D, 0) chops away the negative half of the transformer's 6.3 V_rms output and produces a peak ≈ V_pk − 0.6 V, the addition of a 10 µF (then 100 µF) filter capacitor that holds the peak between cycles and reduces ripple by a factor of ten when the cap is increased tenfold, the ripple formula derivation ΔV ≈ V_max(Δt/RC) ≈ I_load/(fC) where the capacitor discharges through R_load between recharges, the full-wave bridge with four diodes that conducts on both half-cycles (V_in > 0 sends current through one diagonal pair, V_in < 0 through the other) so the output frequency is doubled to 2f = 120 Hz with halved ripple, the diode AND/OR logic where 0 V/5 V inputs combine through forward-biased diodes and a pull resistor to give a HIGH only when both (AND) or any (OR) input is HIGH, and the Zener-equivalent two-diode anti-parallel clipper that limits a 4 V_pp sine to ±0.6 V (or symmetrically clips at higher voltage with multiple diodes in series).

Key equations

$$\text{Forward bias: }V_{D}\approx 0.6\ \text{V (Si),}\ I_{D}\ \text{limited by series }R$$

$$\text{Reverse bias / off: }I_{D}\approx 0,\ \text{diode looks like an open}$$

$$V_{out,\text{half-wave}}(t)=\max\!\big(V_{in}(t)-V_{D},\,0\big)$$

$$V_{rms}=\frac{V_{pk}}{\sqrt{2}}\quad\text{(sinusoidal)}$$

$$V_{pk}=V_{rms}\sqrt{2}\quad\text{(transformer 6.3 V_{rms} → }\sim 8.9\ \text{V_{pk})}$$

$$V_{out,\text{rect}}\approx V_{pk}-V_{D}\quad\text{(after one diode drop)}$$

$$V_{out,\text{bridge}}\approx V_{pk}-2V_{D}\quad\text{(after two diode drops in bridge)}$$

$$I_{load}=\frac{V_{max}}{R}\quad\text{(load current)}$$

$$\Delta V=\frac{I_{load}}{fC}\approx V_{max}\!\cdot\frac{\Delta t}{RC}\quad\text{(peak-to-peak ripple)}$$

$$\Delta t\approx\frac{1}{f}=\frac{1}{60\ \text{Hz}}\approx 16.7\ \text{ms}\quad\text{(half-wave US)}$$

$$\Delta t\approx\frac{1}{2f}\approx 8.3\ \text{ms}\quad\text{(full-wave US)}$$

$$\Delta V_{full}\approx\frac{1}{2}\Delta V_{half}\quad\text{(bridge ripple is half the half-wave)}$$

$$\Delta V\propto\frac{1}{C}\quad\text{(scaling: 10× larger C → 10× smaller ripple)}$$

$$V_{out}=V_{C}(0)\,e^{-t/(RC)}\quad\text{(discharge between recharges)}$$

$$\text{For }RC\gg 1/f:\ V_{out}(t)\approx V_{C}(0)\!\left(1-\frac{t}{RC}\right)$$

$$\text{Diode AND (pull-up): }V_{out}=\min(V_{1},V_{2})+V_{D}\to\text{HIGH only if both HIGH}$$

$$\text{Diode OR (pull-down): }V_{out}=\max(V_{1},V_{2})-V_{D}\to\text{HIGH if either HIGH}$$

$$\text{Voltage clipper: }V_{out}=\begin{cases}V_{D} & V_{in}>V_{D}\\ V_{in} & |V_{in}|<V_{D}\\ -V_{D} & V_{in}<-V_{D}\end{cases}$$

$$\text{Zener regulator: }V_{out}=V_{Z}\quad\text{when reverse current flows through Zener}$$

$$P_{D}=V_{D}\cdot I_{D}\quad\text{(diode dissipation)}$$

$$\text{Forward V across 4 series diodes }=4V_{D}=2.4\ \text{V (clipping ceiling)}$$
