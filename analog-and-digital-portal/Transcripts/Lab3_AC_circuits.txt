Lab 3: AC Response of RC and RLC Circuits
Overview
First lab on AC steady-state response, using the function generator and oscilloscope to measure the gain |V_out/V_in| of an RC low-pass filter, an RC high-pass filter, and an RLC series resonant circuit, plus the input/output phase difference at the −3 dB cutoff. The student fits the measured gain table to the analytic transfer function 1/√(1 + (ωRC)²) for the LP filter (and the analogous expression for the HP and RLC), measures the cutoff frequency f_c = 1/(2πRC) directly from the −3 dB point, and reads the resonance frequency f_0 = 1/(2π√(LC)) and the quality factor Q = f_0/Δf from the RLC peak. This transcript is a page-specific reconstruction for preview and search.

Problem coverage
The set is centered on AC small-signal measurements with R = 9.9 kΩ, C = 10.2 nF for the RC stages and an inductor for the RLC. The pages clearly include the RC low-pass response |V_out/V_in| = 1/√(1 + (ωRC)²), the analytic −3 dB cutoff f_c = 1/(2πRC) ≈ 1.58 kHz with R·C ≈ 1×10⁻⁴ s, the eight-decade frequency sweep (1 Hz to 30 kHz) with V_pp ≈ 1.992 V input and the recorded V_out / V_in ratios that drop from ~1.0 below the cutoff to ~0.05 well above it, the time-delay measurement Δt ≈ 82 µs at f_c = 1.563 kHz giving a phase shift of about 46.3° (close to the theoretical 45° for a single pole), the RC high-pass with the rearranged transfer function |V_out/V_in| = ωRC/√(1 + (ωRC)²) showing the opposite asymptotic behavior, and the series RLC resonance where the peak in |V_R/V_in| picks out f_0 = 1/(2π√(LC)) and the FWHM Δf gives Q = f_0/Δf with sharper peaks at smaller R.

Key equations

$$\left|\frac{V_{out}}{V_{in}}\right|=\frac{1}{\sqrt{1+(\omega RC)^{2}}}\quad\text{(RC low-pass)}$$

$$\omega=2\pi f$$

$$f_{c}=\frac{1}{2\pi RC}\quad\text{(}-3\text{ dB cutoff)}$$

$$\text{For }R=9.9\text{ k}\Omega,C=10.2\text{ nF}:\ f_{c}=\frac{1}{2\pi(9.9\!\times\!10^{3})(10.2\!\times\!10^{-9})}\approx 1.58\text{ kHz}$$

$$\text{At }-3\text{ dB: }\left|\frac{V_{out}}{V_{in}}\right|=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\approx 0.707$$

$$\text{dB}=20\log_{10}\!\left|\frac{V_{out}}{V_{in}}\right|$$

$$\sqrt{2}=\sqrt{1+(\omega RC)^{2}}\Rightarrow 2=1+(\omega RC)^{2}\Rightarrow \omega RC=1$$

$$\Delta\phi=2\pi\frac{\Delta t}{T}\quad\text{(time delay to phase)}$$

$$\Delta\phi(f_{c})=\arctan(\omega RC)=\arctan(1)=\frac{\pi}{4}=45^{\circ}$$

$$\Delta\phi=2\pi(82\!\times\!10^{-6})\,f_{c}\approx 46.3^{\circ}\quad\text{(measured at }f_{c}=1.563\text{ kHz)}$$

$$\left|\frac{V_{out}}{V_{in}}\right|=\frac{\omega RC}{\sqrt{1+(\omega RC)^{2}}}=\frac{R}{\sqrt{R^{2}+(1/\omega C)^{2}}}\quad\text{(RC high-pass)}$$

$$\text{At }\omega\ll 1/RC:\ |H_{HP}|\to 0;\quad \omega\gg 1/RC:\ |H_{HP}|\to 1$$

$$\phi_{HP}=\arctan\!\left(\frac{1}{\omega RC}\right)\quad\text{(high-pass phase)}$$

$$Z_{RLC}=R+i\omega L+\frac{1}{i\omega C}\quad\text{(series RLC)}$$

$$|V_{R}/V_{in}|=\frac{R}{\sqrt{R^{2}+(\omega L-1/\omega C)^{2}}}$$

$$f_{0}=\frac{1}{2\pi\sqrt{LC}}\quad\text{(resonance frequency)}$$

$$Q=\frac{f_{0}}{\Delta f}=\frac{\omega_{0}L}{R}\quad\text{(quality factor)}$$

$$\Delta\omega=\frac{R}{L}\quad\text{(FWHM of resonance peak)}$$

$$\text{At resonance: }X_{L}=X_{C}\Rightarrow Z=R\Rightarrow |V_{R}/V_{in}|=1$$

$$V_{pp}=2V_{0}\quad\text{(amplitude vs peak-to-peak)}$$

$$\Delta t=82\ \mu\text{s},\ T=1/f_{c}\Rightarrow\Delta\phi=2\pi\,f_{c}\,\Delta t\approx 0.808\ \text{rad}\approx 46.3^{\circ}$$

How to use this page
Read each displayed relation as solving one of four jobs: writing the transfer-function magnitude for a single-pole filter and locating its −3 dB point, converting a measured time-delay between input and output traces into a phase, identifying when an RLC reaches resonance by setting the imaginary part of Z to zero, or extracting a quality factor from the FWHM of a resonance peak. That is the logic tying the handwritten algebra together.
