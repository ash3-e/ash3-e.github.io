Key equations

$$\text{Pauli exclusion: }2\,e^{-}/\text{orbital}$$

$$N_{\text{atoms}}\sim 10^{23}\ \Rightarrow\ N_{\text{states}}\sim 10^{23}$$

$$\lambda=\frac{h}{p}\quad\text{(de Broglie wavelength, gives band gaps)}$$

$$k_{B}T/e\approx 25\ \text{mV}\ \text{at room temperature}$$

$$\text{Metal: conduction band partially full}$$

$$\text{Insulator: }\Delta E\gg k_{B}T\ \text{(empty conduction band)}$$

$$\text{Semiconductor: }\Delta E\sim k_{B}T\ \text{(small gap, thermally excitable)}$$

$$\text{N-type: dopants P, As (5 valence }e^{-}\text{)}$$

$$\text{P-type: dopants B, Ga, In (3 valence }e^{-}\text{, "extra holes")}$$

$$E_{C}-E_{V}=E_{G}\quad\text{(band gap energy)}$$

$$I_{sat}\propto e^{-E_{G}/k_{B}T}$$

$$I(V)=I_{sat}\!\left(e^{eV/k_{B}T}-1\right)\quad\text{(Shockley diode equation)}$$

$$\text{Forward bias }V\gg k_{B}T/e:\ I\propto e^{eV/k_{B}T}\ \text{(large }I\text{)}$$

$$\text{Reverse bias }V\ll 0:\ I\to -I_{sat}\ \text{(small)}$$

$$V_{D}\approx 0.6\ \text{V (Si "knee" voltage)}$$

$$\text{Load line: }V_{0}-V_{D}-IR=0\ \Rightarrow\ I=\frac{V_{0}-V_{D}}{R}$$

$$\text{Operating point: intersection of load line with }I(V)$$

$$\text{If }V_{0}<V_{D}:\ I_{D}\approx 0,\ I_{R}\approx 0$$

$$\text{If }V_{0}>V_{D}:\ I_{D}>0,\ V\approx V_{D}\approx 0.6\ \text{V}$$

$$\text{Half-wave rectifier output: }V_{out}(t)=\max\!\big(V_{in}(t)-V_{D},\,0\big)$$
