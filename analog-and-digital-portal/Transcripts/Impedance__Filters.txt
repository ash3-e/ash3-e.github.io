Impedance and Filters
Overview
Single-page lecture notes that build complex impedance from R, C, and L in an AC circuit, then use it to derive low-pass and high-pass filters, the decibel scale, gain as a function of angular frequency on log-log axes, and the resonant RLC voltage-divider with its quality factor and sharp peak. The page emphasises that combining impedances follows exactly the same rules as combining resistors (series add, parallel reciprocal-add) once Z_C = 1/(iωC) and Z_L = iωL have been written down. Key equations

$$\text{Resistor: }V=IR$$

$$\text{Capacitor: }I=C\frac{dV}{dt}$$

$$\text{Inductor: }V=L\frac{dI}{dt}$$

$$v(t)=V_{0}\cos(\omega t),\quad I(t)=I_{0}\cos(\omega t-\phi)$$

$$\hat v(t)=V_{0}\,e^{i\omega t}=V_{0}\big(\cos(\omega t)+i\sin(\omega t)\big),\quad v(t)=\text{Re}\{\hat v(t)\}$$

$$Z_{R}=R,\qquad Z_{C}=\frac{1}{i\omega C},\qquad Z_{L}=i\omega L$$

$$\hat v(t)=\hat I(t)\,Z_{eq}\quad\text{(complex Ohm's law)}$$

$$\text{Series: }Z_{eq}=Z_{1}+Z_{2}\qquad \text{Parallel: }\frac{1}{Z_{eq}}=\frac{1}{Z_{1}}+\frac{1}{Z_{2}}$$

$$\text{Complex magnitude / phase: }Z=A+iB=R\,e^{i\phi},\ R=\sqrt{A^{2}+B^{2}},\ \phi=\arctan(B/A)$$

$$\text{Multiplying by }i\text{: rotates by }+\pi/2\text{ (i.e. }e^{i\pi/2}=i\text{)}$$

$$G_{LP}(\omega)=\frac{V_{out}}{V_{in}}=\frac{Z_{C}}{Z_{R}+Z_{C}}=\frac{1}{1+i\omega RC}$$

$$|G_{LP}|^{2}=\frac{1}{1+(\omega RC)^{2}}$$

$$\omega\ll 1/RC\Rightarrow|G|\to 1\ (\text{low-pass passes}),\qquad \omega\gg 1/RC\Rightarrow|G|\to 0$$

$$G_{HP}(\omega)=\frac{Z_{R}}{Z_{R}+Z_{C}}=\frac{i\omega RC}{1+i\omega RC},\qquad |G_{HP}|^{2}=\frac{(\omega RC)^{2}}{1+(\omega RC)^{2}}$$

$$\text{dB}=10\log_{10}\left|\frac{V_{out}}{V_{in}}\right|^{2}=20\log_{10}\left|\frac{V_{out}}{V_{in}}\right|$$

$$0\ \text{dB}\Leftrightarrow G=1,\ -3\ \text{dB}\Leftrightarrow|G|=1/\sqrt{2},\ -20\ \text{dB}\Leftrightarrow G=0.1$$

$$\phi=\arctan\!\left(\frac{-\omega RC}{1}\right)\quad\text{(LP phase)}$$

$$\text{Slope/falloff: }-20\ \text{dB/decade (single-pole filter)}$$

$$Z_{RLC}=R+\frac{1}{i\omega C}+i\omega L\Rightarrow\text{Im}[Z_{RLC}]=\omega L-\frac{1}{\omega C}=0\ \text{at }\omega_{0}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{LC}}$$

$$Q=\frac{\omega_{0}}{\Delta\omega},\quad \Delta\omega=\frac{R}{L},\quad Q=\frac{\omega_{0}L}{R}$$

$$\left|\frac{V_{R}}{V_{in}}\right|^{2}=\frac{\omega^{2}(\Delta\omega)^{2}}{\omega^{2}(\Delta\omega)^{2}+(\omega^{2}-\omega_{0}^{2})^{2}}$$

How to use this page
Read each displayed relation as solving one of four jobs: rewriting reactive elements as Z_C / Z_L so Ohm's law works in the complex plane, combining those impedances with the same series/parallel rules used for resistors, building a transfer-function magnitude and converting it to dB, or finding the resonance frequency by setting the imaginary part of Z to zero and computing the bandwidth from R/L. That is the logic tying the handwritten algebra together.
