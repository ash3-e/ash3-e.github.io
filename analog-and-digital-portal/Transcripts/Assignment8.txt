Assignment 8
Overview
Homework on op-amp configurations: a voltage-controlled current source built around an op-amp where the load current must be shown to be independent of R_L (then evaluated at V_in = 2 V and at the supply-rail-limited maximum), and an inverting amplifier with a parallel R-C feedback network whose impedance ratio sets the gain so that an AC input of 1 V at ω = 2π × 300 s⁻¹ produces an output of about 4.7 V. Problem coverage
The set is centered on the standard golden-rule analysis of feedback op-amp circuits. The pages clearly include the properties of op-amps (input impedance ≈ 10⁶–10¹⁴ Ω so V⁻ = V⁺ and no input current, output impedance ≈ 1–100 Ω, large open-loop gain A_OL → ∞ inside the unity-gain bandwidth ~10⁵–10⁶ Hz), the transimpedance-style derivation V_L = (5/2)V_in followed by I_out = (V_L − V_in)/R₂ = V_in/R₂ that confirms independence from R_L, the numerical I_out = 10 mA at V_in = 2 V, the maximum-output-V_out = (5/2)V_in + I_out R_L hitting the rail at V_in ≈ 10/3 ≈ 3.33 V, and the inverting amplifier whose gain is the ratio of complex impedances V_out = −(Z₂/Z₁)V_in with Z₂ = R/(1 + jωRC), giving |V_out| ≈ 4.686 V ≈ 4.7 V. The assignment is using these to drill the same workflow: golden rule, virtual short, KCL at the inverting node.

Key equations

$$\text{Golden Rules: }V_{in}^{+}=V_{in}^{-},\qquad I_{1}=I_{2},\qquad I_{\text{input}}=0$$

$$V_{out}=A_{OL}(V_{in}^{+}-V_{in}^{-}),\qquad A_{OL}\approx 10^{4}\text{–}10^{6}$$

$$\text{KCL at inverting node: }\frac{V_{in}-V^{-}}{R_{1}}+\frac{V_{L}-V^{-}}{R_{2}}=0$$

$$\text{With }V^{-}=V_{in}^{+}=V_{in}\ \text{(source on +): }\frac{V_{in}-0}{200}+\frac{V_{in}-V_{L}}{300}=0$$

$$V_{L}=\tfrac{5}{2}V_{in}$$

$$I_{out}=\frac{V_{L}-V_{in}}{R_{2}}=\frac{(5/2)V_{in}-V_{in}}{300}=\frac{(3/2)V_{in}}{300}=\frac{V_{in}}{200}\quad\text{(independent of }R_{L}\text{)}$$

$$I_{out}\big|_{V_{in}=2\,\text{V}}=\frac{2}{200}=0.01\ \text{A}=10\ \text{mA}$$

$$V_{out}=V_{L}+I_{out}\,R_{L}=\tfrac{5}{2}V_{in}+\tfrac{V_{in}}{200}\!\cdot\!100=\tfrac{5}{2}V_{in}+\tfrac{1}{2}V_{in}=3V_{in}$$

$$V_{out,\max}=V_{S}=10\ \text{V}\ \Rightarrow\ V_{in}=\tfrac{10}{3}\approx 3.33\ \text{V}$$

$$\text{Inverting amp gain: }V_{out}=-\frac{Z_{2}}{Z_{1}}V_{in}$$

$$Z_{1}=R_{1}=100\ \Omega$$

$$Z_{2}=\frac{R_{2}}{1+j\omega R_{2}C}\quad(\text{parallel }R_{2}\text{ and }C)$$

$$\frac{|V_{out}|}{|V_{in}|}=\frac{|Z_{2}|}{|Z_{1}|}=\frac{R_{2}/\sqrt{1+(\omega R_{2}C)^{2}}}{R_{1}}$$

$$|V_{out}|=\left|\frac{1/(1+j(2\pi\!\cdot\!300)(1000)(10^{-6}))}{100}\right|^{-1}\!\cdot 1\ \text{V}\approx 4.686\ \text{V}\approx 4.7\ \text{V}$$

$$\omega R_{2}C=(2\pi\!\cdot\!300)(1000)(10^{-6})\approx 1.885$$

$$\sqrt{1+(\omega R_{2}C)^{2}}=\sqrt{1+1.885^{2}}\approx 2.134$$

$$\text{Gain magnitude }=\frac{R_{2}}{R_{1}\sqrt{1+(\omega R_{2}C)^{2}}}=\frac{1000}{100\cdot 2.134}\approx 4.686$$

$$\text{Unity-gain bandwidth: }f_{u}\approx 10^{5}\text{–}10^{6}\ \text{Hz}$$

$$Z_{in}^{(\text{op-amp})}\approx 10^{6}\text{–}10^{14}\ \Omega,\qquad Z_{out}^{(\text{op-amp})}\approx 1\text{–}100\ \Omega$$

$$\text{Saturation: }-V_{S}<V_{out}<+V_{S}$$

$$\text{When }V_{th}>0:\ V_{out}=+V_{S};\quad V_{th}<0:\ V_{out}=-V_{S}\ \text{(no feedback / comparator)}$$

How to use this page
Read each displayed relation as solving one of four jobs: applying the golden rule (virtual short and zero input current), writing KCL at the inverting node, ratioing complex impedances Z₂/Z₁ to get the gain, or checking whether the demanded output exceeds the supply rail.
