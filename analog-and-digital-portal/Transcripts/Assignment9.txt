Overview

Problem coverage
The set is centered on closed-form timing for hysteretic comparators and on basic Boolean enumeration. The pages clearly include the assumed starting point V_out = +V_S with the capacitor initially uncharged at V_C(0) = −βV_S (after the previous flip), the loop equation V_C(t) = V_S + (V_C(0) − V_S)e^(−t/RC) that grows toward +V_S until V_C reaches V⁺ = +βV_S, the algebra βV_S = V_S + (−βV_S − V_S)e^(−t/R_F C) that yields t/RC = ln[(1+β)/(1−β)] for the half-period, the doubling to T = 2R_F C·ln[(1+β)/(1−β)], the substitution β = R₂/(R₁+R₂) giving T = 2R_F C·ln[(R₁+2R₂)/R₁], and the 16-row truth table where Out = 1 only when neither (A,B) nor (C,D) are both 1. The assignment is using these to drill the same workflow: write the RC charging law between two threshold levels, then collapse the answer to a clean log expression.

Key equations

$$\beta=\frac{R_{2}}{R_{1}+R_{2}}$$

$$V^{+}=\beta V_{S}$$

$$V_{C}(t)=V_{S}+\big(V_{C}(0)-V_{S}\big)e^{-t/R_{F}C}$$

$$V_{C}(0)=-\beta V_{S}\quad\text{(value just after the previous flip)}$$

$$\beta V_{S}=V_{S}+(-\beta V_{S}-V_{S})e^{-t/R_{F}C}$$

$$\beta=1-(1+\beta)e^{-t/R_{F}C}$$

$$e^{-t/R_{F}C}=\frac{1-\beta}{1+\beta}$$

$$t_{1/2}=R_{F}C\,\ln\!\left[\frac{1+\beta}{1-\beta}\right]$$

$$T=2R_{F}C\,\ln\!\left[\frac{1+\beta}{1-\beta}\right]$$

$$T=2R_{F}C\,\ln\!\left[\frac{1+\dfrac{R_{2}}{R_{1}+R_{2}}}{1-\dfrac{R_{2}}{R_{1}+R_{2}}}\right]=2R_{F}C\,\ln\!\left[\frac{R_{1}+2R_{2}}{R_{1}}\right]$$

$$\tau=R_{F}C\quad\text{(charging time constant)}$$

$$\text{Schmitt trigger thresholds: }V_{th,\pm}=\pm\beta V_{S}$$

$$\text{AOI logic: Out}=\overline{(A\!\cdot\!B)+(C\!\cdot\!D)}$$

$$\text{De Morgan: Out}=\overline{A\!\cdot\!B}\,\cdot\,\overline{C\!\cdot\!D}=(\bar A+\bar B)(\bar C+\bar D)$$

$$\text{Out}=1\ \Leftrightarrow\ (A\!\cdot\!B)=0\ \text{and}\ (C\!\cdot\!D)=0$$

$$\text{Out}=0\ \Leftrightarrow\ (A=B=1)\ \text{or}\ (C=D=1)$$

$$\text{Truth-table count: out of 16 rows, 9 give Out}=1\ \text{and 7 give Out}=0$$

$$\text{Special rows: }(1,1,*,*)\!\to\!0,\quad (*,*,1,1)\!\to\!0$$

$$\text{If }R_{1}=R_{2}:\ T=2R_{F}C\,\ln(3)\approx 2.197\,R_{F}C$$

$$\text{Duty cycle: }50\%\ \text{(symmetric thresholds }\pm\beta V_{S}\text{)}$$

$$f_{osc}=\frac{1}{T}=\frac{1}{2R_{F}C\,\ln\!\big[(1+\beta)/(1-\beta)\big]}$$
