Overview
Homework on AC reactive networks and complex impedance: combining capacitances in series and parallel, computing the AC voltage across a capacitor and the average power dissipated in the series resistor, decomposing a series RC impedance into real, imaginary, magnitude, and phase parts, and a two-capacitor charge-sharing problem with a resistor in between, including the energy balance. Problem coverage
The set is centered on first-pass AC analysis with phasors. The pages clearly include an equivalent capacitance reduction with mixed series and parallel groupings, an RC voltage-divider driven by a 1 V source where the capacitor voltage and the time-averaged dissipation in R must be found, a complex impedance Z = R + 1/(iωC) with R = 100 Ω, C = 160 nF, ω = 2π × 10 kHz that has to be split into real, imaginary, magnitude, and phase, and finally a switched two-capacitor problem (C₁ initially at V₀, C₂ at zero, single resistor between them) where V_C1(t), V_C2(t), and the total resistive energy loss are required. The assignment is using these problems to drill the same reflex: write Z, then take Ohm's law in the complex plane.

Key equations

$$\frac{1}{C_{eq,\text{series}}}=\frac{1}{C_{1}}+\frac{1}{C_{2}}$$

$$C_{eq,\text{parallel}}=C_{1}+C_{2}$$

$$Z_{C}=\frac{1}{i\omega C}$$

$$X_{C}=\frac{1}{\omega C},\qquad X_{C}=\frac{1}{2\pi(60\,\text{Hz})(0.2\,\mu\text{F})}\approx 5305\ \Omega$$

$$|Z|=\sqrt{R^{2}+X_{C}^{2}}\approx 13210\ \Omega$$

$$V_{C}=V_{0}\cdot\frac{X_{C}}{|Z|}=1\cdot\frac{5305}{13210}\approx 0.4486\ \text{V}$$

$$P_{avg}=I_{rms}^{2}R,\qquad I_{rms}=\frac{I_{peak}}{\sqrt{2}}$$

$$P_{avg}=\left(\frac{V_{0}/|Z|}{\sqrt{2}}\right)^{2}R\approx 3.9\ \mu\text{W}$$

$$Z=R+\frac{1}{i\omega C}=R-\frac{i}{\omega C}$$

$$\text{Re}(Z)=100\ \Omega,\qquad \text{Im}(Z)=-\frac{1}{(2\pi\cdot 10^{4})(160\cdot 10^{-9})}\approx -99.47\ \Omega$$

$$|Z|=\sqrt{R^{2}+\text{Im}(Z)^{2}}=\sqrt{100^{2}+99.47^{2}}\approx 141.05\ \Omega$$

$$\varphi=\arctan\!\left(\frac{\text{Im}(Z)}{\text{Re}(Z)}\right)=\arctan\!\left(\frac{-99.47}{100}\right)\approx -0.7826\ \text{rad}$$

$$C_{eq}=\frac{C_{1}C_{2}}{C_{1}+C_{2}}=\frac{C}{2}$$

$$\text{Initial: }V_{C_{1}}(0)=V_{0},\quad V_{C_{2}}(0)=0$$

$$\text{Charge conservation: }CV_{0}=CV_{1}+CV_{2}\ \Rightarrow\ V_{1}+V_{2}=V_{0}$$

$$V_{C_{1}}(\infty)=V_{C_{2}}(\infty)=\tfrac{V_{0}}{2}$$

$$v(t)=V_{C_{1}}(t)-V_{C_{2}}(t)=V_{0}\,e^{-t/(R C_{eq})}=V_{0}\,e^{-2t/(RC)}$$

$$V_{C_{1}}(t)=\tfrac{V_{0}}{2}+\tfrac{V_{0}}{2}e^{-2t/(RC)}$$

$$V_{C_{2}}(t)=\tfrac{V_{0}}{2}-\tfrac{V_{0}}{2}e^{-2t/(RC)}$$

$$E_{R}=E_{i}-E_{f}=\tfrac{1}{2}CV_{0}^{2}-2\cdot\tfrac{1}{2}C\!\left(\tfrac{V_{0}}{2}\right)^{2}=\tfrac{1}{4}CV_{0}^{2}$$
