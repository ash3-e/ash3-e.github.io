Lab 9: Op-amps with Positive Feedback (Comparator, Schmitt trigger, Oscillator)
Overview
Second op-amp lab on the LF411 (and the LM555 timer IC), this time with positive feedback. The student first builds an open-loop comparator (LF411 with one input grounded) driven by a triangular wave plus simulated noise, sees the comparator's output triggering many false transitions when the noise crosses the threshold, then converts the same circuit into a Schmitt trigger by feeding back a fraction βV_out to the noninverting input so the threshold becomes ±βV_out and the noise has to overcome the hysteresis gap before the output flips. The third part wires a relaxation oscillator (Schmitt trigger plus an R_t C charging branch on the inverting input) with R_t = 100 kΩ, R_1 = 47 kΩ, R_2 = 10 kΩ, C = 0.01 µF, measures the period 0.734 ms (predicted 0.720 ms), and confirms the formula T = 2R_t C·ln[(R_1 + 2R_2)/R_1]. The fourth part wires the 555 in astable mode with R_A, R_B, C selected so T ≈ 0.7(R_A + 2R_B)C and duty cycle D = (R_A + R_B)/(R_A + 2R_B). This transcript is a page-specific reconstruction for preview and search.

Problem coverage
The set is centered on hysteretic comparators and on RC-timed oscillators. The pages clearly include the open-loop LF411 comparator with V⁺ tied to ground showing many spurious zero-crossings whenever the noise amplitude is comparable to the signal slope at the threshold (transition rate climbs from 200 Hz with no noise to many hundreds of Hz with 200 mV noise), the Schmitt-trigger upgrade where positive feedback through a resistor R sets V⁺ = βV_out with β = R_1/(R_1+R) producing two thresholds (turn-on below 0, turn-off above 0) whose separation grows with R until the spurious transitions stop (the lab table records frequencies vs R for noise = 200 mV and 500 mV showing the trigger rate dropping from 1.05 kHz down to 200 Hz as R is increased to ~5 K), the relaxation-oscillator derivation V_C(t) = V_S + (V_C(0) − V_S)e^(−t/RC) with V_C(0) = −βV_S flipping at V_C = +βV_S to give half-period = R_t C·ln[(1+β)/(1−β)] and full period T = 2R_t C·ln[(R_1 + 2R_2)/R_1], the measured T_meas = 0.7342 ms vs T_calc = 0.7196 ms (≈ 2 % agreement), the change of R_t to 20 kΩ that drops T to about 0.144 ms (f ≈ 6.92 kHz), the 555 timer in astable mode where C charges through R_A + R_B from 1/3 V_CC to 2/3 V_CC and discharges through R_B alone giving T ≈ 0.7(R_A + 2R_B)C and the asymmetric duty cycle D = (R_A + R_B)/(R_A + 2R_B) ≈ 0.67 with R_A = R_B, and the observation that the 555 period is independent of V_CC over 5–15 V but does shift when the control voltage on pin 5 is varied between 3 V and V_CC (forming a voltage-controlled oscillator).

Key equations

$$\text{Open-loop comparator: }V_{out}=\begin{cases}+V_{S} & V^{+}>V^{-}\\ -V_{S} & V^{+}<V^{-}\end{cases}$$

$$\text{Schmitt thresholds: }V^{+}=\beta V_{out},\quad \beta=\frac{R_{1}}{R_{1}+R}$$

$$V_{th,\text{high}}=+\beta V_{S}\quad\text{(when }V_{out}=+V_{S}\text{)}$$

$$V_{th,\text{low}}=-\beta V_{S}\quad\text{(when }V_{out}=-V_{S}\text{)}$$

$$\text{Hysteresis width: }\Delta V_{th}=2\beta V_{S}$$

$$\text{Relaxation oscillator: }V_{C}(t)=V_{S}+(V_{C}(0)-V_{S})\,e^{-t/R_{t}C}$$

$$V_{C}(0)=-\beta V_{S}\quad\text{(value at instant of flip)}$$

$$\text{Half-period: }t_{1/2}=R_{t}C\,\ln\!\left[\frac{1+\beta}{1-\beta}\right]$$

$$T=2R_{t}C\,\ln\!\left[\frac{1+\beta}{1-\beta}\right]=2R_{t}C\,\ln\!\left[\frac{R_{1}+2R_{2}}{R_{1}}\right]$$

$$\beta=\frac{R_{2}}{R_{1}+R_{2}}\quad\text{(positive-feedback ratio in the oscillator)}$$

$$\text{For }R_{t}=100\text{ k}\Omega,R_{1}=47\text{ k}\Omega,R_{2}=10\text{ k}\Omega,C=0.01\,\mu\text{F}:\ T\approx 0.72\text{ ms}$$

$$T_{meas}=0.7342\ \text{ms},\quad T_{calc}=0.7196\ \text{ms}\quad\text{(agreement to 2}\%\text{)}$$

$$\text{For }R_{t}=20\text{ k}\Omega:\ T\approx 0.144\text{ ms},\ f\approx 6.92\text{ kHz}$$

$$f_{osc}=\frac{1}{T}=\frac{1}{2R_{t}C\,\ln[(1+\beta)/(1-\beta)]}$$

$$\text{555 astable: }T\approx 0.7(R_{A}+2R_{B})C$$

$$\text{Duty cycle: }D=\frac{R_{A}+R_{B}}{R_{A}+2R_{B}}\quad\text{(}T_{H}\text{ over }T\text{)}$$

$$T_{H}=0.7(R_{A}+R_{B})C\quad\text{(high time)},\qquad T_{L}=0.7 R_{B}C\quad\text{(low time)}$$

$$\text{For }R_{A}=R_{B}=10\text{ k}\Omega,C=22\text{ nF}:\ T\approx 0.464\text{ ms},\ D\approx 0.67$$

$$\text{Charge between }\tfrac{1}{3}V_{CC}\text{ and }\tfrac{2}{3}V_{CC}\Rightarrow t_{H}=R_{eff}C\,\ln 2\approx 0.693R_{eff}C$$

$$\text{Period independent of }V_{CC}\ (\text{ratio cancels})$$

$$f_{osc}^{(555)}=\frac{1}{0.7(R_{A}+2R_{B})C}\quad\text{(astable frequency)}$$

How to use this page
Read each displayed relation as solving one of four jobs: writing the open-loop comparator output as ±V_S based on the sign of V⁺ − V⁻, dressing the inverting input with positive feedback to introduce a hysteresis gap that filters noise, computing the period of an RC-charged Schmitt oscillator from the time it takes V_C to swing between the two thresholds, or applying the 555-timer ratio formulas T ≈ 0.7(R_A + 2R_B)C and D = (R_A + R_B)/(R_A + 2R_B) for an astable oscillator. That is the logic tying the handwritten algebra together.
