Lab 4: Transmission Lines and Impedance Matching
Overview
Lab on the high-frequency behavior of an RG-58U coaxial cable (nominal Z_0 = 50 Ω): measuring the per-100-ft attenuation in dB by sending a 1 V_pp sine through ~500 ft of terminated cable, observing how reflections off a non-matched termination cause pulse echoes after a propagation delay 2L/v, identifying the open-circuit (Γ = +1, doubling) and short-circuit (Γ = −1, inversion) limits, then measuring Γ from echo amplitudes to extract Z_L from a known mystery termination using Γ = (Z_L − Z_0)/(Z_L + Z_0). The student also estimates the pulse propagation speed v from the round-trip delay and compares it to c/n with n ≈ √k_R for the polyethylene dielectric. Key equations

$$\alpha[\text{dB}]=20\log_{10}\!\left(\frac{V_{out}}{V_{in}}\right)\quad\text{(attenuation in dB)}$$

$$\alpha[\text{dB/100 ft}]=\frac{\alpha[\text{dB}]}{L/100\,\text{ft}}\quad\text{(per-length normalisation)}$$

$$Z_{0}=\sqrt{L_{0}/C_{0}}\quad\text{(characteristic impedance)}$$

$$Z_{0}^{\text{coax}}=\frac{1}{2\pi}\sqrt{\frac{\mu_{0}}{\varepsilon_{0}\varepsilon_{r}}}\,\ln\!\left(\frac{d_{1}}{d_{2}}\right)\quad\text{(coax geometry)}$$

$$\Gamma=\frac{V_{R}}{V_{in}}=\frac{Z_{L}-Z_{0}}{Z_{L}+Z_{0}}\quad\text{(voltage reflection coefficient)}$$

$$\text{Open: }Z_{L}\to\infty\Rightarrow\Gamma=+1\quad\text{(doubled amplitude)}$$

$$\text{Short: }Z_{L}=0\Rightarrow\Gamma=-1\quad\text{(inverted reflection)}$$

$$\text{Matched: }Z_{L}=Z_{0}\Rightarrow\Gamma=0\quad\text{(no echo)}$$

$$Z_{L}=Z_{0}\,\frac{1+\Gamma}{1-\Gamma}\quad\text{(solve for unknown load)}$$

$$v=\frac{1}{\sqrt{L_{0}C_{0}}}=\frac{c}{n},\quad n=\sqrt{\varepsilon_{r}}\quad\text{(propagation speed)}$$

$$T_{rt}=\frac{2L}{v}\quad\text{(round-trip time)}$$

$$v\approx\frac{2}{3}c\quad\text{for polyethylene dielectric coax}$$

$$\text{Pulse speed: }v=\frac{2L}{T_{rt}}\quad\text{(extracted from echo timing)}$$

$$P_{in}=\frac{V_{0}^{2}}{2Z_{0}}\quad\text{(time-averaged incident power)}$$

$$P_{ref}=|\Gamma|^{2}P_{in}\quad\text{(reflected power)}$$

$$P_{trans}=(1-|\Gamma|^{2})P_{in}\quad\text{(transmitted power)}$$

$$\Delta\phi=2\pi\,f\,\Delta t\quad\text{(time-of-flight to phase)}$$

$$\frac{V_{out}}{V_{in}}=10^{-\alpha L/(20\cdot 100\,\text{ft})}\quad\text{(amplitude after L feet)}$$

$$T_{rise}\sim 0.35/B\quad\text{(rise-time bandwidth product)}$$

$$\lambda_{cable}=\frac{v}{f}\quad\text{(wavelength on cable, with reduced }v\text{)}$$

How to use this page
Read each displayed relation as solving one of four jobs: converting amplitude ratios to dB and dB/100 ft, calculating a reflection coefficient Γ from the echo-to-input ratio, inverting that Γ back into a load impedance, or timing round-trip echoes to extract the propagation speed v on the cable. That is the logic tying the handwritten algebra together.
