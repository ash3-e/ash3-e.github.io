Transmission, Impedance, and Reflection
Overview
Single-page lecture notes that derive coaxial-cable transmission lines from a chain of small LC segments, set up traveling-wave solutions V(x,t) = V_0 cos(kx − ωt), recover the wave equation ∂²V/∂x² = (1/c²)∂²V/∂t², compute the characteristic impedance Z_0 = √(L_0/C_0), evaluate it for a coaxial geometry (Z_0 ≈ 50 Ω lab cable, 75 Ω cable TV), then move to reflections at a load Z_L using the boundary conditions for V and I and finishes with PHYS0525 transmission-line practice problems where Γ = (Z_L − Z_0)/(Z_L + Z_0) is applied for matched, mismatched, open-circuit, and short-circuit terminations. Key equations

$$Z_{R}=R,\qquad Z_{C}=\frac{1}{i\omega C},\qquad Z_{L}=i\omega L$$

$$\hat v=\hat I\,Z,\qquad v(t)=\text{Re}\{\hat v\},\qquad I(t)=\text{Re}\{\hat I\}$$

$$\text{Series: }Z_{eq}=Z_{1}+Z_{2}\qquad \text{Parallel: }\frac{1}{Z_{eq}}=\frac{1}{Z_{1}}+\frac{1}{Z_{2}}$$

$$V_{n-1}-V_{n}=i\omega L_{0}\frac{\partial I_{n}}{\partial x},\qquad I_{n+1}-I_{n}=i\omega C_{0}\frac{\partial V_{n}}{\partial x}$$

$$\frac{\partial V}{\partial x}=L_{0}\frac{\partial I}{\partial t},\qquad \frac{\partial I}{\partial x}=C_{0}\frac{\partial V}{\partial t}$$

$$\frac{\partial^{2}V}{\partial x^{2}}=L_{0}C_{0}\frac{\partial^{2}V}{\partial t^{2}}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \frac{1}{L_{0}C_{0}}\frac{\partial^{2}V}{\partial x^{2}}=\frac{\partial^{2}V}{\partial t^{2}}$$

$$V(x,t)=V_{0}\cos(kx-\omega t),\qquad k=\frac{2\pi}{\lambda},\ \omega=2\pi f$$

$$c=\frac{\omega}{k}=\lambda f=\frac{1}{\sqrt{L_{0}C_{0}}}$$

$$c=3\!\times\!10^{8}\ \text{m/s}=1\ \text{ft/nanosecond}\quad\text{(vacuum)}$$

$$L_{0}=\frac{\mu_{0}}{2\pi}\ln\!\left(\frac{b}{a}\right),\qquad C_{0}=\frac{2\pi\varepsilon_{0}}{\ln(b/a)}$$

$$Z_{0}=\sqrt{\frac{L_{0}}{C_{0}}}=\frac{\ln(b/a)}{2\pi}\sqrt{\frac{\mu_{0}}{\varepsilon_{0}}}\approx 50\ \Omega\ (\text{lab})$$

$$Z_{0}=\sqrt{\frac{L_{0}}{C_{0}}}=\sqrt{\frac{\mu\mu_{0}}{\varepsilon\varepsilon_{0}}}\cdot\frac{\ln(b/a)}{2\pi},\qquad k_{R}\approx 2\text{–}3\ \text{for cable insulation}$$

$$\text{For cable TV: }Z_{0}=75\ \Omega\quad\text{(propagation speed reduced by }\sqrt{k_{R}}\text{)}$$

$$\text{Long cable looks like resistive load with }R=Z_{0}\quad\text{(characteristic impedance interpretation)}$$

$$V_{0}=V_{in}+V_{R},\qquad I_{0}=\frac{V_{in}}{Z_{0}}-\frac{V_{R}}{Z_{0}}\quad\text{(boundary conditions at load)}$$

$$\Gamma=\frac{V_{R}}{V_{in}}=\frac{Z_{L}-Z_{0}}{Z_{L}+Z_{0}}$$

$$\text{Open circuit (}Z_{L}\to\infty\text{): }\Gamma=+1\quad\text{(reflected wave in phase, }V_{0}\text{ doubled at end)}$$

$$\text{Short circuit (}Z_{L}=0\text{): }\Gamma=-1\quad\text{(reflected wave inverted, }V_{0}=0\text{ at end)}$$

$$\text{Practice 1: }Z_{0}=50\ \Omega,\ Z_{L}=75\ \Omega\Rightarrow \Gamma=\frac{75-50}{75+50}=\frac{25}{125}=\frac{1}{5}$$

$$\text{Practice 2: }Z_{L}=R+i\omega L+\frac{1}{i\omega C},\ \text{notch at }\omega=\frac{1}{\sqrt{LC}}\ (\Gamma=0)$$

$$\text{Practice 3: }P_{in}=\frac{V_{0}^{2}}{Z_{0}},\ P_{ref}=|\Gamma|^{2}P_{in}=0.04\ P_{in},\ P_{trans}=0.96\ P_{in}$$

$$V_{load}=\sqrt{Z_{L}\,P_{load}}=\sqrt{(0.96\!\times\!10^{-3})(75)}\approx 0.268\ \text{V}\quad\text{(RMS, P_in = 1 mW)}$$

How to use this page
Read each displayed relation as solving one of four jobs: deriving the wave equation by chaining the inductor and capacitor of an infinitesimal cable segment, dressing the resulting traveling wave with a characteristic impedance Z_0 = √(L_0/C_0), enforcing matching of voltage and current at a load to extract the reflection coefficient Γ = (Z_L − Z_0)/(Z_L + Z_0), or auditing power so that incident equals reflected plus transmitted along the line. That is the logic tying the handwritten algebra together.
