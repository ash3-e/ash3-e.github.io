Key equations

$$I_{d,\text{sat}}=K\,(V_{GS}-V_{p})^{2}$$

$$\sqrt{I_{d,\text{sat}}}=\sqrt{K}\,(V_{GS}-V_{p})$$

$$\text{Slope of }\sqrt{I_{d,\text{sat}}}\text{ vs }V_{GS}=\sqrt{K}$$

$$\text{x-intercept of }\sqrt{I_{d,\text{sat}}}\text{ vs }V_{GS}=V_{p}$$

$$\sqrt{I_{d}(0)}=\sqrt{K}(0-V_{p})\ \Rightarrow\ 3=-\sqrt{K}\,V_{p}$$

$$\sqrt{I_{d}(-2)}=\sqrt{K}(-2-V_{p})\ \Rightarrow\ 1=-2\sqrt{K}-\sqrt{K}V_{p}$$

$$\Rightarrow\ V_{p}\approx -3,\qquad K\approx 1$$

$$\text{Tabulated values: }V_{GS}=0\!:\!I_{d}=9,\ -0.5\!:\!6,\ -1.0\!:\!4,\ -1.5\!:\!2,\ -2.0\!:\!1,\ -2.5\!:\!\sim 0.2\ \text{(mA)}$$

$$\sqrt{I_{d}}\ \text{values: }3,\ 2.49,\ 2,\ 1.41,\ 1,\ 0.45$$

$$\text{Ohmic region: }0\le V_{DS}\le |V_{p}|\approx 2.5\ \text{V (at }V_{GS}=-1\text{ V)}$$

$$R_{DS}=\frac{V_{DS}}{I_{D}}\bigg|_{\text{ohmic}}\approx\frac{2.5\ \text{V}}{9\ \text{mA}}\approx 250\ \Omega$$

$$\text{Constant current source: }I_{D}=I_{d,\text{sat}}(V_{GS})\ \text{independent of }V_{DS}$$

$$\text{Tying gate to source: }V_{GS}=0\ \Rightarrow\ I_{D}=9\ \text{mA}\ \text{(at }V_{GS}=0\text{)}$$

$$I_{L}=I_{D}=9\ \text{mA}$$

$$V_{DS,\text{sat}}\approx 2.5\ \text{V}\ \text{at }V_{GS}=0$$

$$V_{R_{L},\max}=V_{S}-V_{DS,\text{sat}}=10-2.5=7.5\ \text{V}$$

$$R_{L,\max}=\frac{V_{R_{L},\max}}{I_{D}}=\frac{7.5\ \text{V}}{9\ \text{mA}}\approx 833\ \Omega$$

$$g_{m}=\frac{dI_{D}}{dV_{GS}}=2K(V_{GS}-V_{p})\quad\text{(transconductance)}$$

$$\frac{dI_{D}}{dV_{DS}}\bigg|_{\text{ohmic}}=\frac{1}{R_{DS}}$$

$$\Delta V=|V_{p}|+V_{BS}\quad\text{(channel voltage with body bias)}$$
