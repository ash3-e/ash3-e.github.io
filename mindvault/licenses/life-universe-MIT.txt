Life Universe by saharan — https://oimo.io/works/life/
Source: https://github.com/saharan/works/tree/main/life

Mindvault ports the original recursive OTCA metapixel simulation and WebGL shader,
including losslessly decoded numeric lookup data, from commit
f34a4951a95ba178bfc9964d08dc898795ea4736. Life runs at 0.025x; camera zoom traverses
one 2048-fold recursion every eight active seconds. The reference website's
controls, icons, input controller and bundled Haxe runtime are not included.
