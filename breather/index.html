Inside the breather

A localized wave that oscillates without dispersing. This explorer connects the sine–Gordon breather to its folded, pseudospherical surface.

φ(x, t) = 4 atan [ a sin(ωt) / (ω cosh(ax)) ]
ω = √(1 − a²)   ·   φtt − φxx + sin φ = 0

Surface follows a moving window along the surface’s time parameter. Space–time plots the field; the bright cross-section marks the current time.

Boomerang eases forward and backward through a finite time interval. Variation smoothly modulates the shape parameter. Set it to zero for a fixed exact breather; changing it explores a family of solutions, rather than solving a driven wave equation.

Vectors show normalized surface normals or time tangents. In space–time, displacement arrows show φ and velocity arrows show its partial derivative ∂φ/∂t with shape held fixed; their display lengths are scaled.
